## Rulings on your S1 concerns (they change the design; build them first)

1. **Journal header (your concern 1; amendment A10).** The journal is no longer created empty. Its first line is a header, written with the exclusive create and flushed in the same step: `{"journal":1,"createdAt":<ms>}` followed by a newline. The journal's age is measured from the **later** of the header's `createdAt` and the file's creation time; both must be at least 60 seconds old. A journal without a valid header as its first line refuses every purchase ("The purchase record is damaged; nothing was bought.") and is left as it is. The header never counts as a claim and contains no quote id. The clock for `createdAt` is the injected one.
2. **A used quote stays used when the journal has lost its line (your concern 2; amendment A11).** Before the claim: if an outcome file or a result file exists for the quote, readable or not, the quote is never claimed and the request is answered as "already claimed" (5.4) from what is on disk. Put the guard back. The mutation evidence changes accordingly: with the claim step skipped, tests 2, 4 and 5 must still fail with more than one submit, and a new test ("the journal lost the quote's line, the outcome file is there: the repeat buys nothing and reports the job") must fail when the guard is removed. Show both runs.
3. **Units (your reading 15).** The three units are `krea_units` (a number, may be fractional, shown as "units"), `usd_micros` (a whole number of millionths of a US dollar, shown as dollars with two to four decimals, for example `$0.7021`) and `callirra_credits` (a whole number, shown as "credits"). Rename `usd`. Any other unit: the entry counts as switched off.
4. **A job is reported whatever its entry's switch says (your reading 6).** A request about a quote that is already claimed, and a `status` request, report the job even when the entry has been switched off since. Only quoting and buying need the entry to be on.
5. **Dots (your reading 12).** While a job is followed, a dot is written every 10 seconds by the injected clock whether or not a status check is in flight. If that needs a second injected timer function, add it to `clock` and say so in the report.
6. The nonce from `crypto.randomBytes`, the 78.5-bit ids, the order of checks, full job ids and the rest of your readings stand.
