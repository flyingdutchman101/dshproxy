## 2. What this implies for a personal router

- Replicate deletes API outputs after an hour, Alibaba after 24 hours and Runway after 24 to 48 hours. **Inference:** the router must download each result on success and keep its own history.
- Runway keeps credits on `SAFETY.INPUT.*`, Higgsfield charges Grok at start, while Krea, fal (5xx) and Replicate (aborted) charge nothing. **Inference:** store a per-route "refusal is charged" flag and show it at confirmation.
- Entry ceilings are 1–2 (Runway tier 1) and 2 (fal); Runway, fal and Krea Pro queue the excess server-side, while Kling and Krea without backlog reject it. **Inference:** a local queue with one or two slots per route is enough; a scheduler is not justified.
- Three of Runway's five recent third-party-model incidents lasted five to fifteen hours, and Runway says of `THIRD_PARTY.UNAVAILABLE` "You should not retry these errors immediately". **Inference:** automatic in-lane retry buys little; a manual "resend on another route" with a new confirmation is the useful fallback.
- Stated ceilings are long: 2 hours (Krea external), 3600 s (fal default), 30 minutes (Replicate), and relaxed modes state none. **Inference:** the router needs its own deadline and a cancel-while-queued action; Krea documents that as free, fal says the request is "never processed", and Replicate does not charge aborted predictions.
- Krea has "no public endpoint for checking your balance", and its backlogged jobs fail with `insufficient_balance` without a webhook. **Inference:** a local spend ledger and cap are worth building; always poll.
- fal retries up to 10 times and falls back to equivalent endpoints by default; Alibaba says "Do not create duplicate tasks". **Inference:** with no idempotency key, client retry risks double charges; keep one send per job.
- Runway and Higgsfield limit simultaneous jobs in unlimited modes and promise no wait time. **Inference:** treat unlimited routes as draft lanes with no deadline guarantee.

## 3. Practice accounts

- fal [reliability](https://fal.ai/docs/documentation/model-apis/inference/reliability.md): queued requests are retried on 503, 504 and connection errors "up to 10 times"; model fallbacks "are enabled by default for all accounts"; opt out per request with `X-Fal-No-Retry` and `x-app-fal-disable-fallback`.
- Runway [Model Routers](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/model-routers/): the router picks a model by cost, latency or quality under "a maximum spend per generation"; each response reports the model and the cost. The overview describes selection, not retry after failure.
- Kling [concurrency rules](https://kling.ai/document-api/api/get-started/concurrency-rules.md) recommend exponential backoff (initial delay at least 1 second) and a client-side task queue.
- I found no post-mortem or engineering blog from a company running video generation in production.
